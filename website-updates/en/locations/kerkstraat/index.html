What goes into your Philly?

The Classic Philly Cheesesteak combines premium ribeye, caramelized onions and cheddar or mild provolone on a freshly baked roll. Explore the Motown with mushrooms and paprika, or the Medley with black truffle.

A sandwich or the full combo

On our website, the Classic costs €15. The €20 Beef Cheesesteak Combo includes a beef cheesesteak, French fries and your choice of drink. The Chicken Combo costs €18. Optional sauces cost €1 each.

FAQ

Where is this location?

Kerkstraat 55, 2060 Antwerp, Belgium

When is this branch open?

Tuesday–Sunday 16:00–22:00. Closed Monday. Holiday hours may differ.

How do I choose this branch when ordering?

Open the order page, choose pickup or dine-in and select Kerkstraat. Your requested time needs restaurant confirmation.

Can I have lunch here?

Kerkstraat is scheduled to open at 16:00 Tuesday–Sunday. Check Oude Beurs hours for lunch.

Are chicken and vegetarian options available?

Yes. The menu includes Philly Chicken, Basil Chicken and Truffled Forest Chicken. The vegetarian Vegaco contains king oyster mushrooms, onions, paprika and cheddar or mild provolone. Ask about ingredients and cross-contact for dietary needs or allergies.

How quickly can I collect my order?

Choose an available pickup time when ordering. This is a request that needs restaurant confirmation; no fixed waiting time is promised.

Are all menu items halal-certified?

Yes. The owner confirms that all menu items at both locations, Kerkstraat and Oude Beurs, are halal-certified. Contact the restaurant for information about the certification.

Can I order allergen-free food?

Halal certification does not mean a dish is allergen-free. Contact the restaurant before ordering about ingredients and cross-contact. This page does not guarantee allergen-free preparation.
